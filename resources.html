Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions answered by our Certified Public Accountants.

The EOPT Act (RA 11976) introduced major reforms: it established a unified Sales Invoice for both goods and services (replacing Official Receipts for VAT input claim), categorized taxpayers into Micro, Small, Medium, and Large for tailored compliance, and allowed digital filing/payment across any BIR Revenue District Office.
Under BIR regulations, corporations and individuals with gross quarterly sales or receipts exceeding ₱3,000,000 are required to have their financial statements audited by an independent CPA. Submission to the BIR occurs alongside the Annual Income Tax Return (Form 1702/1701) by April 15 following the fiscal year, followed by filing with the SEC via SEC eFAST.
