Who We Are

About GPR Accounting & Consultancy Services

A premier Philippine accounting and business advisory firm committed to giving business leaders the power of timeliness, financial clarity, and regulatory peace of mind.

Our Founding Purpose

Empowering Philippine Businesses through Timely Financial Advice

Founded by veteran Certified Public Accountants in the Philippines, GPR Accounting & Consultancy Services was created to eliminate the traditional gap between accountants and business owners.

We recognize that running a successful business in the Philippines requires navigating complex Tax Code provisions, BIR regulations, SEC mandates, and local government compliance. Our philosophy centers on **"The Power of Timeliness"**—delivering actionable financial insights before critical deadlines so our clients can make proactive growth decisions.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accurate, transparent, and timely accounting, tax, and advisory services that enable Philippine MSMEs and corporations to thrive sustainably.

👁️ Our Vision

To be the premier, most trusted accounting and business consultancy partner recognized across the Philippines for excellence and integrity.

Deep Regulatory Expertise

Ease of Paying Taxes (EOPT) Act

Full implementation of standardized invoicing, digital BIR filing, and taxpayer classification systems.

CREATE & TRAIN Act Compliance

Strategic tax planning maximizing lowered corporate income tax rates & PEZA/BOI fiscal incentives.

SEC & Corporate Governance

Beneficial Ownership Transparency, Annual Corporate Returns, and internal compliance auditing.
